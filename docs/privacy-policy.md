# Earloom privacy policy

Source text for the privacy policy page on affluentlabs.dev, which the app
stores link to. Earloom is made by Affluent Labs LLC.

**Short version: Earloom collects nothing.** There are no accounts with us,
no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your phone

Your servers and sign-in tokens (in the system's secure storage), the books
you import, your downloads, your listening history and stats, your settings.
None of it is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

## What goes to your own server

When you connect an Audiobookshelf server, Earloom talks to that server only:
it signs in, reads your library, streams and downloads your books, and
syncs your progress, bookmarks and listening sessions. That server belongs
to you or to whoever runs it; its own privacy rules apply there.

## Things the system provides

- Lock screen, Android Auto, CarPlay and widgets show what you are playing.
  This data stays on your devices.
- Notifications are local: downloads and new podcast episodes, only if you
  turn them on.
- If you share a year in review card, only that image leaves your phone, and
  only to where you share it.
