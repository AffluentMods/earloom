# Earloom support

Source text for the support page on affluentlabs.dev, which the App Store and
Google Play listings link to.

## Get help

- Email: contact@affluentlabs.dev
- Bugs and ideas: https://github.com/AffluentMods/earloom/issues

When something goes wrong, Earloom shows what happened and a "Copy details"
button. Paste those details into your email or issue: they say what failed
without any password or token.

## Common questions

**Do I need an Audiobookshelf server?**
No. You can add audiobook files to Earloom with "Add files" and listen with
no server at all. To stream your own library, you need an Audiobookshelf
server (https://www.audiobookshelf.org) that you or someone you trust runs.

**Earloom cannot reach my server.**
Check the address works in the phone's browser first. Use the full address,
including `https://` and any port, for example `https://books.example.com`
or `http://192.168.1.20:13378`. On iPhone, allow Local Network access in
Settings, Earloom, if the server is on your home network.

**Can I listen offline?**
Yes. Tap the download button on a book or episode. Downloaded books play
with no connection, and your progress syncs the next time Earloom reaches
your server.

**Where is my progress kept?**
Progress for server books is saved on your server, so it follows you to
other devices and apps. Progress for books you added as files stays on your
phone.

**Does Earloom collect my data?**
No. There are no accounts with us, no analytics and no ads. See the privacy
policy.

Earloom is an independent app for Audiobookshelf servers. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project.
