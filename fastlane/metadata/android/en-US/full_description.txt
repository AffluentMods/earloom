Earloom is a free, open source audiobook player. Listen to the library on your own Audiobookshelf server, or to audiobook files you copy onto your phone. No ads, no account with us, no tracking.

<b>Your server, your library</b>
• Sign in to your Audiobookshelf server with a password or single sign-on.
• Home, Library, series, authors, collections and playlists, with search across all of it.
• Progress syncs both ways, and when you pick up a book you started on another device, Earloom offers to jump there.
• Podcasts: new episodes, playback that remembers where you were, and automatic downloads.

<b>Or just files on your phone</b>
• Add M4B, MP3 and other audiobook files. Earloom reads titles, authors, chapters and covers.
• Books on this phone work exactly like server books, and need no server at all.

<b>Made for listening</b>
• Each book's cover colors the player and the book page.
• Chapters, bookmarks, speed from 0.5x to 3.5x, remembered per book, and skip times you choose.
• A sleep timer that fades out, can stop at the end of a chapter, turns itself on at bedtime, and gives you more time when you shake the phone.
• A short rewind after a pause, so you never lose the thread.

<b>Offline first</b>
• Download books and episodes and play them with no connection. Your progress waits and syncs when you are back online.

<b>Everywhere you listen</b>
• Android Auto, lock screen and notification controls, Bluetooth buttons, a home screen widget and app shortcuts.
• Listening stats: time per day, streaks, your books, and a year in review.
• Light, dark and pure black themes, large text support, and layouts for tablets.

<b>Private by design</b>
Earloom has no analytics, no crash reporting services and no ads. Your data goes only between your device and your own server.

Earloom is an independent app for Audiobookshelf servers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project. Source code: https://github.com/AffluentMods/earloom
